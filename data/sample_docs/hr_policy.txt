Human Resources Policy and Guidelines

1. Standard Core Working Hours
Standard working hours are flexible between 08:00 and 17:00 local time, Monday through Friday. Core collaborative hours are expected between 10:00 and 15:00.

2. Hybrid and Remote Work Policy
Eligible team members may participate in the hybrid work arrangement, allowing up to two days per week of remote work upon approval from their direct supervisor. Remote workstations must maintain secure internet connectivity.

3. Annual Leave and Paid Time Off (PTO)
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Vacation requests should be submitted through the HR portal at least two weeks prior to the requested start date.

4. Professional Learning Stipend
Employees are eligible for an annual professional development budget to support conference attendance, technical certifications, and relevant training courses.
